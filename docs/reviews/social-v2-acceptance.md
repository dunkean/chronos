# Social v2, lot S5: acceptance walk on generated data

English is the source. A short French summary closes the document.

This is the walk of design §18.4, run with Playwright against the real host (`/api/v2`) on a world generated by the pinned Lineage Lab worker. Nothing here comes from the hand-written fixture: the banner is hidden, the data are served by `chronos-app serve` and the network calls are `POST /api/v2/worlds/{canon}/query`, `POST /graph`, `GET /explain/{id}` and `GET /export`.

## Limits observed

- The mayor of aldermoor in 1360 (Person f777) has a parent and a child but no friend circle at that date; the walk on the default site cannot pass step 5. This is data, not a rendering fault. The circle route works on dunmarch, larkfield and others.
- Two groups of the same site can share a name and an ordinal ("Guild 0 of aldermoor"), because the fallback name is kind, ordinal and site; the ordinal is per archetype.
- Several mayors of the first run (aldermoor 1360) changed after the S4 fixes of main; IDs are stable within a canon, not across S4 revisions.
- A group row of another site appears in the timeline when a resident of the displayed site belongs to it (recruitment budget of 14 days). The map shows where its members live.
- Only the domains requested are materialized: the other domain of the region shows "packet not computed" on the map and in the table. A view never depends on what else is in memory.
- The playback of this walk is a script run by hand through the Playwright tools; it is not part of `cargo test`.

## Résumé en français

Le parcours de recette du §18.4 a été joué avec Playwright sur un monde réel généré (seed 11, `canon-df5bbacd929d7790bb3bf9af4fe38378`), via `/api/v2`, bannière de fixture masquée. Les 13 étapes passent sur le site `dunmarch` : région ouverte, 1360, maire, famille et appartenances développées, cercle d'amis, implantations d'une guilde sur la carte, remontée d'une crise à sa cause, 1340 et retour à la même empreinte, rechargement aux mêmes IDs, attendu et réalisé, carte agrandie avec contacts extérieurs nommés mais jamais placés, export, puis réouverture par import sur un autre serveur sans relancer le fournisseur. Sur le site par défaut (aldermoor), le maire de 1360 n'a pas de cercle d'amis à cette date : l'étape 5 y échoue, ce que le document consigne comme une limite des données.
